For phone and tablet: sign in to the portal, view shifts clearly and sync them to your device calendar when you choose.

How it works

Three steps to get started

  1. 1

    Download and sign in

    Install IVU Plus from Google Play or the App Store, open the app and sign in to the WebCrew portal with your credentials. You can save multiple profiles securely on your device.

  2. 2

    View your shifts

    Browse the monthly calendar, daily details, rest days and changes. The dashboard and home widget show today’s shift at a glance.

  3. 3

    Sync if you want

    Turn on optional sync with your phone calendar for reminders and alignment with your personal schedule. You can disable it anytime in settings.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does IVU Plus replace the WebCrew portal?

No. IVU Plus complements the official portal with a more convenient mobile interface. You still access WebCrew systems, but view shifts and details more easily on phone or tablet.

Is it an official Trenitalia or Ferrovie dello Stato app?

No. IVU Plus is an independent tool by Leo Granata. It is not approved or operated by Trenitalia or Ferrovie dello Stato. Portal use remains subject to the operator’s policies and your employment terms.

How does calendar sync work?

If enabled, the app copies shifts, rest days and absences to your device system calendar. You choose which calendars to use and can turn sync off anytime. If your calendar syncs to cloud services (Google, iCloud), their privacy policies apply.

Are my data and passwords secure?

Credentials are protected on device with secure storage and encryption. Shift data stays mostly local. See the full privacy policy (Italian).

Can I use multiple accounts?

Yes. Save several profiles on the same device and switch between them, with credentials encrypted locally.

Does the app work offline?

You can view already downloaded shifts without a connection. Portal access, updates and sync require an active connection.

Which devices are supported?

IVU Plus is available on Android (Google Play) and iOS/iPadOS (App Store), for phones and tablets.

How do I get support?

Email leogranata@gmail.com for questions and assistance. If you enable “Error reporting” in Settings, the app can send optional technical reports to help improve stability.
